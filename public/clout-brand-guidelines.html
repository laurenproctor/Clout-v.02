01   Brand Positioning

Where raw ideas
become refined thought.

Clout exists at the intersection of spontaneous insight and editorial polish. We transform fragmented thinking into coherent narratives without losing the original spark.

Brand Promise

Your scattered thoughts deserve a second act. We capture the brilliance in your voice notes, text fragments, and casual observations—and transform them into content that positions you as the expert you already are.

Core Philosophy

Great thought leadership isn't manufactured. It's extracted, refined, and synthesized from what you're already thinking. Our role is editorial, not inventive—finding signal in noise.

Target Audience

Executives, consultants, and subject matter experts who have insights worth sharing but lack the time or inclination to package them. People who think in fragments but need to publish in paragraphs.

Brand Personality

Editorial without being stuffy. Sophisticated without being pretentious. We're the thoughtful editor who sees what you meant to say and helps you say it better.

04   Synthesis Aesthetic

The visual philosophy
behind everything we make.

Our design system embodies synthesis—the union of organic thinking and structured expression. Every visual decision reinforces this central tension.

Editorial First

Layout, typography, and spacing follow editorial design principles. Think magazine spreads, not SaaS dashboards. Generous whitespace. Confident hierarchy. Room to breathe.

Warmth Over Clinical

Cream, not white. Warm near-black, not pure black. Soft, low, warm-tinted shadows. The brand should feel human and considered—analog texture in a digital product.

Two Darks on Cream

Olive for action and brand, slate for depth and feature bands. Restraint is the system: no gradients, two background colors total, content does the talking.

Restrained Animation

Motion exists only to communicate state or guide attention. Subtle fades and gentle rises on scroll—eased, never bouncing. Never call attention to cleverness.

The synthesis aesthetic isn't decoration. It's the visual manifestation of our core value proposition: taking fragmented raw material and producing coherent finished work. Every design choice should reinforce this transformation.

05   Voice & Tone

Write like an editor,
not a marketer.

Our voice is confident without being performative. We acknowledge the messiness of real thinking while demonstrating the clarity that comes after synthesis. We love the rule of three: "Real reach. Real relevance. Real clout."

Write this way

Your best ideas are already there. We just help you find them.

Turn voice notes into LinkedIn posts. Coffee-shop conversations into newsletter content. Shower thoughts into strategic insights.

Thought leadership isn't manufactured. It's extracted from what you're already thinking.

Never write this way

Tone principle: We're the editor who sees what you meant to say. Respectful of the raw material. Confident in our ability to refine it. Never condescending, never overpromising. The work speaks louder than the marketing.

06   Photography & Illustration

Real people, real moments.
Never stock lifestyle.

Photography should feel editorial—captured, not staged. Warm, natural light. Paired with the brand's hand-drawn marker illustrations (the "stress scribbles," the golden-ratio doodle) that add analog, human texture.

✓ Good

  • • Editorial portraiture with natural, warm lighting
  • • People at work, mid-thought, engaged
  • • Authentic environments—offices, cafes, studios
  • • Hand-drawn marker illustration for texture
  • • Muted, warm, natural color grading
  • • Real professionals in their element

✗ Avoid

  • • Stock photos of people pointing at laptops
  • • Overly posed "lifestyle" imagery
  • • Diverse-team-in-meeting-room clichés
  • • Cold, blue-grey or over-saturated grading
  • • Generic "entrepreneur" aesthetics
  • • Emoji in UI (the footer 💙 is the only exception)

07   Layout Principles

Structure guides the eye.
Whitespace does the work.

Our layouts borrow from editorial design—classical balance, generous margins, content that breathes. Every page should feel considered, never cluttered.

Container

Content maxes at 1200px; hero copy in a ~760px column. Centered and generous. Big vertical rhythm between sections.

Spacing Scale

4px base unit. Stack: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px. Section padding runs 64–128px vertical.

Eyebrows

Sections open with an uppercase, 14%-tracked eyebrow, usually with a trailing arrow (Capture →). The one pill-shaped element in the system.

Cards & Shadows

Softly-squared rectangles (~10px radius), warm hairline borders, soft low warm-tinted shadows—never harsh black. On slate bands, cards are outlined rather than filled.
